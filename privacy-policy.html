🧸 Cozy Match · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026 · Developer: Honeypaw Studios · Türkçe

In short: The game itself does not collect personal data; your progress is stored only on your own device. The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, AdMob collects some device data, as described below.

1. Data the game collects

The game does not request, read or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or similar personal data. There are no game accounts. Signing in to Google Play Games is optional (see section 5).

2. Game data stored on your device

To make the game work, the following is kept in your device's local storage:

  • Level progress, stars and Daily Puzzle results
  • Your bear's level, outfits, room decorations, the dancing cactus and seasonal event progress
  • Power-ups, achievements, the daily chest and ad frequency counters
  • Settings (sound, music, vibration, theme, language, daily reminder time) and which tips have been shown

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this information from your device (a cloud save, if you made one, stays in your Google account; see section 5).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using AdMob by Google LLC. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect the following data and share it with Google:

  • Device and account identifiers: Android Advertising ID, app set ID
  • IP address: may be used to estimate your device's approximate location
  • App interactions: such as app launches, taps and video views
  • Diagnostics: information about the performance of the app and the SDK

This data is encrypted in transit. To learn how Google uses this data, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices: In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent to personalized ads when you first open it. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Rewarded ads ("📺 Watch") are entirely optional; the whole game can be played without watching them.

4. Gameplay statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To find levels that are too hard and to fix crashes, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics by Google LLC. They receive:

  • Gameplay events: level number, mode, stars, number of moves and time when a level starts, ends or is lost
  • Crash reports and diagnostics: error messages, device model, Android version and app version
  • Identifiers: a random app instance ID created by Firebase (not your name or email)

Your name, email, save code or any text you type are never sent. This data is encrypted in transit and used only to improve the game. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

5. Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, your unlocked achievements, your total stars (leaderboard) and a copy of your game progress (cloud save) are stored in your Google account by Google. Your Play Games profile name may be visible to others on the leaderboard. You can sign out or delete your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app at any time.

6. In-app purchase ("Remove ads")

The game offers one optional one-time purchase that turns off interstitial ads. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; the developer never receives your card or payment details. The game only learns whether the purchase is owned. In-game stars cannot be bought with real money.

7. Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder in Settings, the game shows one local notification a day at the time you choose. The notification is created on your device; no notification data is sent to a server. You can turn it off in the game or in Android settings.

8. Internet connection

The game can be played offline. The internet is used only for ads, the services described in sections 4–6, and nothing else.

9. Save backup code and sharing

The "Copy code" button in Settings turns your progress into a text code. It only contains game progress and goes nowhere unless you copy and paste it yourself.

When you tap "Share" in the Daily Puzzle, only your result (day number, stars, time, moves) is handed to your device's share menu or clipboard as text. You choose where it goes.

10. Permissions

  • Internet: for ads, statistics, crash reports, Play Games and purchases.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for ads.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on the daily reminder.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback in the game; can be turned off in Settings.

No camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage permission is requested.

11. Children

The game itself does not ask users of any age for personal data. The game is intended for users aged 13 and over.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
